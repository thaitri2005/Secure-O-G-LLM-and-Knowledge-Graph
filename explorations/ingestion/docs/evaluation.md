# Evaluation

The criteria were fixed before scoring. Methods for form pairing and header cleaning were frozen before held-out page selection. Development results are identified below; no independent held-out evidence is claimed for the prototype's review-informed header-only extension. All inference was local, with CPU parsing or an Intel Arc Vulkan endpoint and models at most 7B. [Input pins](../configs/inputs.lock.json), [source annotation conventions](../annotations/README.md).

## Parser comparison

Question: does a local VLM justify replacing the standard corpus parser? The registered criterion retains standard parsing when its nine-table arithmetic mean cell accuracy is no more than five percentage points below the VLM, and lists every VLM win of at least ten points. Grid cells include empty covered anchors and use the unchanged repository whitespace-exact positional scorer (`src/graph_builder/ingestion/scoring.py:44`). Extra row/column counts are reported separately.

Fast TableFormer averages **85.1%**, accurate **68.9%**, and the local MinerU 1.2B Q8 VLM **72.7%**: retain fast. The bit-summary table is the only VLM win over fast of at least ten points: **91/234 versus 10/234** cells, but its 31 columns still differ from 26 annotated columns. Fast loses the last abbreviation anchor; the VLM keeps it. These are small English samples, not a general parser ranking. [Scores and wrong cells](../results/parser-comparison/scores.json), [semantic source review](../results/parser-comparison/source-value-review.json), [native snapshot replay](../experiments/parser_comparison.py).

## Hard-page selection

Question: can source/parser signals cover every observed VLM win and the two daily-report cases within a 15% page budget? Three formulas were registered using rotation, orientation, OCR mode, table size, empty columns, available confidence and label/value counts. The input contains **788 pages from 49 documents**; unavailable table confidence never triggers a low-confidence condition.

The broad, moderate and constrained rules select **453/788 (57.5%)**, **429/788 (54.4%)** and **139/788 (17.6%)** pages and catch **3/5, 3/5 and 1/5** required pages: **all fail**. Every rule misses the fluid-parameters page and AK-3 abbreviations. Rotation also selects the 68-32 cover, approval sheet, contents and prose. Those are possible unnecessary calls; they were not scored as VLM false positives. [Measured signals](../results/hard-page-selection/signals.csv), [selections and visual observations](../results/hard-page-selection/scores.json), [replay](../experiments/hard_page_selection.py).

## VLM text support

Question: does same-page token support reject an invented theorem fragment while false-flagging at most 10% of visually correct pages? Pre-registered page thresholds are .90/.95/.98; span rules require zero support, at least four tokens with zero support, or at least four tokens with support at most .25. Source normalization is the repository token function (`src/graph_builder/ingestion/evaluation.py:20`).

All nine threshold/rule combinations **fail**. The correct cohort has nine pages; combined false flags range from 2/9 to 7/9, and no setting drops the theorem fragment. Its words can appear elsewhere on the same page, so lexical support does not establish factual association. The daily header has 96.6% unique-token support despite the material added fragment. Visual labels are subjective and single-annotator. [Page/span results, literal fragments and threshold outcomes](../results/text-support/scores.json), [source text](../results/text-support/references.json), [replay](../experiments/text_support.py).

## Continued tables

Question: can geometric/header rules preserve logical tables and attach their headings on each continuation part without wrong joins? The exact-header rule joins **0/4 development positive boundaries** across two tables and two native variants and has one reviewed wrong join. The aligned-column rule reaches **4/4** development boundaries with reviewed precision 100%, but polluted/missing logical headers fail the complete criterion. Header cleaning keeps those decisions and fixes all development headers: **development pass**. [Exact-header evidence](../results/continued-tables/exact-header-development.json), [aligned-column evidence](../results/continued-tables/aligned-column-development.json), [header-cleaning evidence](../results/continued-tables/header-cleaning-development.json).

On the frozen held-out set, both aligned-column variants join **0/3** positive boundaries and fail heading attachment. Two other predicted joins remain unreviewed, so precision is unknown. The coordinates table starts with only `Item / Plan / Actual`; the activity table has incompatible native 8×12, 7×14 and 5×3 grids. The label-only fragment is incorrectly treated as furniture, and rotated activity headings fail similarity or mapping checks. [Held-out aligned-column result](../results/continued-tables/aligned-column-held-out.json), [held-out header-cleaning result](../results/continued-tables/header-cleaning-held-out.json).

The completed prototype adds upright retries and the header-only extension. It joins both development tables (98.4% and 95.5% data cells, header on every part) and the 16A coordinates table (100%), but neither boundary of the 68-32 activity table (0/2, 0.2% data cells): the upright retry is rejected on p30 because word recall drops. Boundary recall, cell accuracy, header attachment and examples are in [the unified evaluation](../results/evaluation.json) and [generated table](../results/evaluation.md). **The extension is informed by those held-out failures; these current scores are descriptive.** Precision is reported only for reviewed boundaries, with corpus-wide precision left unknown for unlabeled predictions.

## Form pairing

Question: can visible source glyphs and vector cell boundaries associate labels with values without numeric invention? The frozen template uses label vocabulary and value direction only, without stored values or page coordinates. Criteria require at least 90% exact-pair accuracy on each form, zero semantic invented values and complete bbox presence. Exact-pair accuracy is multiset intersection divided by multiset union, so extra predictions are penalized.

The template matches **141/141 development** and **93/93 held-out** pairs. The generic method reaches about 61%, so it fails. Four held-out literal source-absent flags are printed ellipses stored separately in the PDF; source review finds zero semantic inventions. There are no wrong template pairs to quote on this sample. Generic errors and source review remain in [development results](../results/form-pairing/development.json), [held-out results](../results/form-pairing/held-out.json) and [ellipsis review](../results/form-pairing/semantic-review.json).

The prototype selects the template by visible-label share and marks generic output unverified. [Current evaluation](../results/evaluation.json) reports pair accuracy, literal source-absent values, bbox presence, overlap coverage against reviewed key/value boxes and mean matched IoU. Presence alone does not prove correct grounding. The two held-out forms and three development forms are too small for a general form-extraction claim.

## Scanned-page OCR

Question: can English OCR preserve three permit-page transcriptions and at least 28 of 33 critical tokens? The engine comparison selects OCRmyPDF with **29/33** critical tokens. Tesseract inside the corpus converter retains **20/33**, below the minimum. OCRmyPDF followed by text-mode conversion retains **26/33 raw** and fails the original exact-token threshold. The direct OCR layer retains 29/33; three added raw mismatches are apostrophes in `2600’`, `70’` and `10’` becoming ASCII quotes. [Engine comparison](../results/scanned-page-ocr/engines.json), [converter Tesseract](../results/scanned-page-ocr/converter-tesseract.json), [text conversion](../results/scanned-page-ocr/text-conversion.json).

The unified evaluation reports **raw and normalized** scores. Typographic single quotes and primes (`’ ‘ ′`) map to ASCII apostrophe; double quotes and double primes (`” “ ″`) map to ASCII quotation mark. Whitespace is collapsed and case retained. This changes matching policy, not source annotations or extracted text. Critical tokens, CER, WER and order-independent word recall are reported per page and in aggregate. The completed prototype retains **26/33 raw and 29/33 normalized** critical tokens. Normalized prose CER is 17.7% on the letter (p23) and 4.1% on its continuation (p24), against 5.7% and 0.6% for the OCRmyPDF sidecar text alone, so the text-mode conversion adds layout and line-joining differences. CER on the scanned form is diagnostic because its cell reading order is conventional; prose CER is reported separately. [Current OCR results and missed tokens](../results/evaluation.json).

Concrete lexical errors in the tool survey include `API 5L X42` → `API SL X42`, `800` → `B00`, and `Fax:` → `Fox:`. Output volume is not accuracy: word counts are retained separately. The letter/schematic examples and each tool's three observed errors are in [tool evidence](../results/tool-survey.json).

## Resume, identity and structural checks

The required corpus checks use AK-3 plus 44 appendices (492 pages) and five main/extended reports (325 pages). Schema validity, unique IDs and resolving references, source-span bounds, canonical text offsets and complete extracted-page reconstruction are checked independently of accuracy. Two conversion directories start without shared native caches; a third process tree is deliberately killed and resumed. The comparison uses exact canonical `SHA256SUMS`, with elapsed times external and serialized geometry rounded to 0.01 PDF point. [Run results, cache hashes/timestamps, throughput and identity verdicts](../results/pipeline-checks.json).

Native access violations occurred in long-lived conversion processes. The same affected source pages converted successfully in a bounded diagnostic. The completed runner isolates each document in its own process, propagates failures without automatic retries, and reuses completed caches during recovery. This mitigates accumulated native process state; the underlying native failure is not proven. The earlier failures remain part of the run evidence. No uninterrupted-success claim is inferred from recovered work.

Reproduce all current annotation metrics with [one evaluation command](usage.md). The retained historical parser/selection/support measurements have separate replay modules; current table/form/OCR scoring shares [the package evaluator](../src/ingest_prototype/evaluate.py). [Final checks](../results/checks.json) record byte-for-byte evaluation reproduction, tests, annotation preservation, links, trace exceptions and the reviewable inventory.
