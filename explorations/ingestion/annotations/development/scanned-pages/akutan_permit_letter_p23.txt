Dear Mr. Scanlan:

Enclosed is the approved application for permit to drill the above referenced geothermal
exploratory well.

This permit application is approved contingent upon the following conditions:
1. Well operations shall be conducted in accordance with 20 AAC 25.
2. Pursuant to 20 AAC 25.035 (h) (1), alternate BOPE is approved.
3. Pursuant to 20 AAC 25.035 (h) (2), the diverter requirement is waived.
4. Pursuant to 20 AAC 25.061 (c), the near surface survey requirement is waived.
5. Pursuant to 20 AAC 25.066 (d) (3), alternate gas detection equipment is approved.
At least one methane detector and one H2S detector are required.
6. Test BOPE to 750 psi on a seven-day frequency.
7. Submit all LOT charts/data with final 10-407 report.
8. Core chips, core descriptions, core photographs and core analysis, if any analysis are
conducted, are required to be submitted to the AOGCC per 20 AAC 25.071.
9. Flow or injection tests: pressure, temperature and flow charts and data (both
qualitative and quantitative) recorded during every flow/injection test are required to
be submitted to the AOGCC per 20 AAC 25.071.
10. Inclination Survey shall be conducted in the well to determine the inclination from
vertical with surveys starting at 500 feet and no more than 500 feet apart to total
depth per 20 AAC 25.050.
11. Fluid Samples: Geochemical analysis, if acquired, are to be submitted to the AOGCC
per 20 AAC 25.071.

Pursuant to 20 AAC 25.071(b)(2), dry cuttings samples must be submitted to the Commission.
The preferred sample interval is 10’, with a maximum of 30’.
