# BSEE LLM-annotated benchmark v2

re-sealed for publication; label content identical

Publication freeze supersedes the archival freeze chain. Labels were produced by two independent LLM annotators from different model families and adjudicated by an LLM; they are not human expert labels. The adjudicator shares annotator_a's family. Time is unscored, and v1 and v2 scores are not comparable.

The benchmark contains 1,057 adjudicated facts: 727 G+, 82 G-, 100 G?, and 148 other. The test split contains 155 documents (150 readable), with L2 inter-annotator F1 of 0.8538622129436325. Agreement does not establish correctness. Among partial disagreements, the adjudicator selected annotator_a 91 times and annotator_b 27 times, and edited 9; this preference and the shared model family are limitations. Adjudication retained 15 agreed facts whose time annotations differed; time remains unscored. Date normalization follows the frozen guideline, including its policy for unlisted date forms.

The protocol introduction was reworded for publication to use neutral annotator roles; annotation rules are unchanged. The publication freeze and seals bind this introduction.

The lexicon source reference now points to the publication annotation guide. Its rules and literal entries are unchanged; the v2 publication freeze and seals were renewed for this metadata change.
